About CR7

Cristiano Ronaldo dos Santos Aveiro

Cristiano Ronaldo is a Portuguese professional footballer widely regarded as one of the greatest players in football history.

Born on 5 February 1985 in Funchal, Madeira, Ronaldo began his professional career with Sporting CP before moving to Manchester United.

His career has included major spells at Manchester United, Real Madrid, Juventus and Al Nassr. He is known for his goal scoring, athleticism, powerful shooting and dedication to training.
